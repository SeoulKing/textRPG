# 1층 벽·바닥 파츠 원화 v3

- 방식: Codex 내장 이미지 생성. PixelPen 트레이싱을 위한 검토용 원화이며 게임에 들어가는 PNG가 아닙니다.
- 주 참조: [사용자 시안 부위별 확대](../first-floor-material-study.png).
- 보조 참조: 보관된 실내·실외 타일셋과 정사영 기하 가이드의 비교판.

```text
Create ONE beautiful production concept plate for the Academy Ground Floor architecture in a hand-pixeled top-down 2D fantasy RPG. The concept will be put under transparent PixelPen layers and deliberately redrawn pixel by pixel as individual components. This image is the concept for review, not a finished map or game atlas. Use the academy material study image as the primary specific look reference; use the other reference sheet only for the game's fixed front-orthographic projection, readable proportions and paint finish, never copy its tiles or elements. Preserve the direction, richness and warm material palette of the academy study. 

Large landscape sheet with 3 wide columns and 3 rows, nine separated specimens on a single flat muted midnight-purple backdrop, ordered from left to right. Each specimen is large, framed only by negative space, separated by wide blank gutters, not touching any other. No text, numbers, decorative borders or characters anywhere. Match all specimens' pixel scale. Draw each at a completely fixed, directly front-facing 3/4 top-down orthographic view, axes vertical and horizontal, no isometric diamonds, no vanishing points. Floors are flat rectangular swatches in screen space; wall top faces are visibly broad bands from the top, same projection throughout. 
1. Warm pale pink-beige limestone hall tile swatch, about 6x5 tiles, elegant clipped corners and small dark intersections, clear fine grout, irregular subtle color, delicate pixel clusters of worn highlights and aged pores. 
2. Warm honey-brown wooden classroom boards, vertical running planks, staggered joints and fine occasional knots, small cuts and slight wear, vintage golden caramel, distinct handcrafted variation.
3. Dark lavender-grey laboratory slate swatch with subtly chipped and bevelled edges, deep joints, muted lavender variations, smooth working surfaces, no objects.
4. Rear wall straight module and its readable cutaway end: broad dark plum charcoal coping seen from ABOVE with fine edges and joints, warm sandstone interior courses below, aged rose mortar and tiny variation, fine projecting horizontal stone base. Keep its front perfectly horizontal. No window or lamp to obstruct the material.
5. Two long side-wall coping profiles, mirrored for left and right, clearly seen from ABOVE as broad flat dark plum cut stone ribbons running vertically in the image, not a stacked front elevation; intentional small highlights, chipping and texture, slim visible inner edge. 
6. South/front cutaway segment: broad dark upper coping visible from above and a clearly separate shadowed umber brick plinth beneath facing the viewer, richly modeled thickness and warm edge bounce. One short capped end at each side.
7. Left and right ninety-degree corner joins and jamb/end caps, two or three independent pieces, using exact same coping finish and wall thickness as panels 4 through 6. Each piece has clean contact geometry.
8. Door threshold modules: a wide warm wooden step with 2-3 shallow treads, and a separate light worn sandstone sill of the same width. View from above, centered and unobstructed, no door or walls attached.
9. Small in-context demonstration of the same materials connecting in an OPEN doorway: adjacent bright flagstone and honey boards, wall cap and contrasting inner face, both side jambs, shallow wooden sill, no door leaf, no objects. Its purpose is to demonstrate seam connection.

Beautiful hand-placed clusters, sharply defined square pixels, small palette ramps per material, colored selective contours, controlled high-frequency details only at stone edges, woodgrain and seams. The finish should match a polished boutique pixel-art RPG, architectural masonry with weight and character rather than a clean or generic vector grid. No photographic textures, gradients, bloom, oversharpened noise, perspective floor, decorative fantasy glyphs, clutter, or furniture. Use soft warm daylight bounce just within each specimen; no exterior cast shadows. No full-room composition.
```
