Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: One original environment concept image for Arcana, a cozy pixel-rendered 3D life sim. This is the visual target for a later modular Blender and Godot build.
Primary request: Design an inviting personal home in a quiet magical town, with a working kitchen, living room, and separate bedroom under one coherent architectural shell.
Composition: A single wide landscape illustration, centered house viewed with Arcana’s orthographic three-quarter game camera, facing the front square-on with a shallow downward tilt around 30 degrees. Frame the entire house with a little breathing room. The roof and nearest exterior plane are intentionally cut away as one continuous clean dollhouse opening. Behind that opening, the building is real and connected. Back and side walls meet in clearly built corners. Two continuous lime-plaster and dark-timber partition walls actually join the structure and each has a generous open doorway with lintel, wooden door jambs, thickness, and a continuous threshold. Put the openings on readable sight lines so the player can see the kitchen and bedroom through the doorways. Show complete interior wall spans between doors. A continuous little front sill and timber base give the whole model a clean deliberate cut line. Avoid disconnected wall stumps, missing wall slices, floating beams, exposed gaps between wall and floor, and a warehouse-like open floor plan.
Layout and props: A comfortable living room by the front entry with a muted plum sofa, warm lamp, low table and woven blue-gold rug. A separate working kitchen at the back left with a pale limestone cooking hearth, practical oak chopping counter, pottery, herbs and two small food bowls. A separate bedroom at the back right with a warm oak single bed and deep blue quilt, a bedside lamp and a small bookcase. Clearly readable walkable path from entrance through living room to both framed doorways. Props feel individually modelable, with natural contact shadows, practical scale and space around legs and trim. A handful of restrained brass and celestial motifs hint at Arcana's town.
Visual style: Handcrafted stylized 3D environment art, tactile beveled geometry, subtle fine pixel finish like a professionally pixel-rendered Blender diorama rather than flat 2D illustration. Honey oak, cream lime plaster, dark chestnut structural timber, muted midnight blue textiles, dusty plum, brushed brass. Late afternoon light through blue-tinted windows mingles with soft amber hearth glow. Warm light, soft clean shadow shapes, gently lit corners. Dark neutral backdrop. Original design. No characters, typography, UI, callouts, other views, logos, or watermarks.
